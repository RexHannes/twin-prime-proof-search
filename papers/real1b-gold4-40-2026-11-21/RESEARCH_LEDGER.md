## Exact source relationships (no double-counting)

Set \(a(n)=\mathbf1_{X<n\le2X}\vartheta_{\mathbb P}(n-2)\),
\(R_z(n)=\mathbf1_{P^-(n)>z}\) with \(z=(2X)^{1/4}\), and
\(\ell_B=\alpha_XR_z-w_X\), \(0<\alpha_X\le1\), \(w_X\ge0\), \(w_XR_z=0\).
For rough \(n\) all auxiliary small-prime marks \(\mathcal W(n)\) vanish.

- Signed rough mass: \(\sum a\mu R_z=-T+C_2-C_3\).
- Unsigned rough mass: \(\sum a\mu^2R_z=T+C_2+C_3\).
- Complete identity: \(A_{\rm sf}-\mathcal E_{\mathcal W}-\delta+2W_o=2\alpha_X(T+C_3)\).

The weighted rough tangent can be reindexed as \(-T_b-\sum_{p>z}H_p^{(b)}\), using the unique least prime factor of each squarefree composite. This is **not** independent twin credit.

## Method-specific failures (do not over-generalize)

**Absolute Cauchy.** Put \(W_0=W_o+W_e\), \(M=\sum a\mu^2|\ell_B|\),
\(V=\sum a\mu^2|\ell_B|(1-\mathcal W)^2\), and
\(D_w=\sum a\mu^2w_X(1-\mathcal W)^2\).
Then \(M=A_{\rm sf}+2W_0\), \(V=A_{\rm sf}+W_0+D_w\), and
\(\sqrt{MV}\ge A_{\rm sf}\) (assuming \(A_{\rm sf}\ge0\)). The *method's upper bound* is too large. It says nothing about the sign of the actual residual.

**BV transfer.** J's direct cofactor-dependent partial summation gives a term of the reported order \(X^2/(P(\log X)^{A+1})\) for \(P=X^t\), \(1/3<t<1/2\); it does not become \(o(X/\log X)\) at fixed precision. An entirely different new dispersion estimate is not ruled out.

**Large-mark transplant.** Four prime marks each \(>(2X)^{1/4}\) have empty simultaneous support on \(n\le2X\). This blocks direct transplantation of the many-small-mark strategy, not any future one- or two-large-mark theorem.

**Polynomial roughness.** A zero ordinary-integer average for \(g_\eta\) does not remain zero after inserting \(R_z\); X reports a nonzero \(X/\log X\)-scale main term requiring exact centering.
