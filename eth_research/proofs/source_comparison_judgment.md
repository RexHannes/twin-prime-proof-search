## Source comparison

Fix \(0<a<1\) before the limit \(x\to\infty\). The physical quantity in both certificates is
\[
O=\sum_{x/2<n\le x}R_{\exp((\log x)^a)}(n-2)\mu^2(n-2)
C_{x^{.24},x^{.49}}(n-2)(\Phi_x-\psi_x)(n).
\]
The same original \(C\) sums every eligible prime owner including its full signed cofactor-divisor sum. The same \(\Phi-\psi\) has the original good, retained, rough and squarefree output selectors. The same 1,566 five-prime cells with the same six slice integrals of the nonnegative height and displaced faces define \(\psi\) and \(J\). Zero-output and zero-owner cases are covered by these definitions. In particular the native coefficient of a prime \(k=n-2\) is zero, while genuine primes enter the separate transferred actual boundary \(A(\psi)\). The \(S_b\) used in both certificates is the same physical comparator.

Let \(U,U_B\) be the native whole and box rows, \(T\) the log-weighted twin mass, and \(A(\psi)\) the true-prime box mass. The identities \(O=U-U_B\) and \(U_B=A(\psi)-JS_b+o(S_b)\) give
\[
\frac{O+T+A(\psi)}{S_b}
=\frac{U+T}{S_b}+J+o(1).
\]
The upper-direction whole-source estimate drops \(A(P)\ge0\) once, with its native good-positive sign. Both certificates use that same whole-source estimate and the same rank-two, rank-four, tail, calibrated-mean, comparator and prime-power payments. Removing \(T\) and \(A(\psi)\), each once, is legitimate on the upper side because \(S_b>0\) eventually and both physical populations are nonnegative.
