# An obstruction for nonpositive owner rows under a scalar smooth prefix

Let v>0, A>0 satisfy A+v<1/2, and let h be smooth from the right at zero, h(0)=1 and h(t)=0 for t≥A. Put B=1−v. In the smoothed Vaughan sum with owner prime ℓ>x^v, the inner tail for a nonunit squarefree cofactor carrying exponents t_i is the negative of its Boolean finite difference

    G_h(t_1,…,t_m) = Σ_{S⊆[m]} (−1)^|S| h(Σ_{i∈S}t_i).

Consider the proposed sufficient pointwise sign G_h≥0 for all such exponent profiles with Σt_i<B. This quantifier is a relaxation independent of the shifted output. Near-coincident exponents may be obtained by continuity from distinct exponents. Ordinary PNT in the individual fixed exponent intervals and one final dyadic prime interval realizes arbitrary interior limits for input rows if desired. It does not control the factorization of the shifted output.

**Slope theorem.** This pointwise condition forces −h′(0)≥3/(2A). The unavoidable comparator charge of this device exceeds 3 units per unit of the ordinary positive profile mass.

Indeed, for w=1−h the two-coordinate differences state w(s+t)≤w(s)+w(t) whenever s,t>0 and s+t<B. Splitting t into m equal pieces and sending m→∞ gives w(t)≤tw′(0+). For four increments just above A/2 their total remains less than B, because 2A<1−v. Every subset of size ≥2 lies outside the support, so G_h=1−Σ_i h(t_i)≥0. Sending all t_i↓A/2 yields w(A/2)≥3/4. The two estimates imply the asserted slope bound. With the genuine prime boundary included, the principal term relating this smoothed sum to the original contains the additional multiple (1−v)(−h′(0)) of the actual comparator. The harmonic Möbius transform and its linear term at zero are derived in the companion Mellin note. In particular (1−v)·3/(2A)>3. The unchanged prime-side upper already costs 7/3 on positive ordinary profile mass. Universal pointwise sign by this mechanism cannot improve that coefficient.

**Quantified failure if a smaller slope is chosen.** If all pair differences are nonnegative and (1−v)(−h′(0))≤7/3, then by the same pair test h(A/2)≥1−7A/(6(1−v)). As 2A<1−v, the four-label G_h near A/2+ is strictly below −2/3 in the limit. By continuity an open four-label region has strictly positive raw owner weight there. This proves a geometric counterexample to the proposed pointwise simplification. It does not assert that any native joint f-positive shifted population has positive lower density. If a proof avoids the four-label deficit by violating the pair condition, its two-label signed deficit must be paid instead.

The hypothesis is intentionally specific. Signed joint estimates, additional label-dependent annihilators and native output-dependent restrictions can evade it. No bound on the unknown signed remainder is inferred from this theorem.
