\section{Open analytic problems}\label{sec:openproblems}

The current state is best advanced by attacking the physical residual rather
than by weakening the status firewall.

the Fourier image of the labelled six-prime target, the five-prime rough

\classification{\openstatus}{Source-exact route refinement.}
\begin{problem}[Beyond full-band diagonal energy]\label{prob:covariance}
Find a covariance estimate coupling the cardinal transform $G_i(\tau)$ to the
progression error $E_i(\tau)$ that improves on the absolute-energy capacity
\cref{eq:cardinal-capacity}.  A bound for $G$ and $E$ separately is
insufficient when $K\le R$.
\end{problem}

$\mu(d_0+At)$ and the sampled six-prime sequence
$\beta_{6,C}(B_0+kt)$, uniformly in the determinant family.  The theorem must
retain the paired high-pass target rather than replace it by a fixed bounded
sequence.
\end{problem}
