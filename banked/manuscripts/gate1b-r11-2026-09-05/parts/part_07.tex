\begin{equation}\label{eq:cardinal-capacity}
             \left(\frac RK+\frac{D}{KP}\right)^{1/2}.
\end{equation}
For $K\le R$, the first term is at least one.  Parseval alone therefore does not
close the full spectrum; additional covariance between $G_i$ and $E_i$ is
required.

\subsection{Moving-shift Chowla}

The physical M\"obius shift is $s=Ah$.  Since $RT=D$ and

$DX^{-o(1)}$ distinct shifts.  Shift scarcity is not the obstruction.  The
available averaged Hardy--Littlewood--Chowla framework

        \mu(d_0+At)
        \quad\text{and}\quad
        \beta_{6,C}(B_0+kt)
\]
averaged over $A,k$, with the determinant and paired high-pass target retained.
A source-compatible progression/target mixed-moment theorem remains unproved.

\subsection{Factoring the five-prime modulus}

Write $A=pa$ with $(p,a)=1$.  Solve first
$kd\equiv2\pmod a$ by $d=d_*+au$.  The missing prime imposes
\[
       u\equiv u_p:=(2-kd_*)\overline{ka}\pmod p,
\]
so exactly
\begin{equation}\label{eq:factorA}
 \1_{kd\equiv2\, (A)}
  =\1_{kd\equiv2\, (a)}\frac1p
    \sum_{j\bmod p}\eord{p}{j(u-u_p)}.
\end{equation}
Keeping only $j=0$ changes the source.  Estimating every $j$ independently does
not automatically recover the gain expected from the shorter modulus $a$.
The source also admits a distinct one-prime chart, but a high target frequency
in the present chart is not automatically low in that chart; an exact
cross-chart projector comparison is missing.

\subsection{Automorphic completion and the constant spectrum}

The matrix identity \cref{eq:matrix-hecke} places the source in one Hecke cell.
Under the standard normalisation for the trivial-character Hecke operator
\cite{Iwaniec2002},
\begin{equation}\label{eq:hecke-constant}
                    T_h\mathbf1=\frac{\sigma_1(h)}{\sqrt h}\mathbf1.
\end{equation}
Suppose a source-preserving completion produces
\[
             F=\sum_t\mu(d_t)r_t\ph_t
\]
on a finite-volume quotient with $\int\ph_t=1$.  Its constant-spectrum
contribution to the complete Hecke quadratic form is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:constant-mass}
 \boxed{
 \frac{\sigma_1(h)}{\sqrt h\,\operatorname{vol}}
      \left|\sum_t\mu(d_t)r_t\right|^2.
 }
\end{equation}
Although $\sum_tr_t=0$ for a centred target, this does not imply
$\sum_t\mu(d_t)r_t=0$.  Thus target mean zero does not justify deleting the
automorphic constant.  The automorphic-kernel estimates presently inspected
\cite{GrimmeltMerikoski2025} control completed source functionals by positive
autocorrelation norms; they do not supply the missing bound for
\cref{eq:constant-mass} in this physical tensor.

\subsection{Gcd descent and Kloosterman fractions}

Let
\[
 g=(d_t,d_{t+h})=(d_t,h),
 \qquad d_t=gd',\quad d_{t+h}=gd'',\quad h=gh'.
\]
Then $(d',d'')=1$ and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gcd-desc-det}
                  d'B_{t+h}-d''B_t=2h'.
\end{equation}
Consequently,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:kloosterman-congruences}
 B_{t+h}\equiv2h'\overline{d'}\pmod{d''},
 \qquad
 B_t\equiv-2h'\overline{d''}\pmod{d'}.
\end{equation}
These are genuine Kloosterman-fraction phases.  However, after the high-$g$
strata are removed, the source still contains two M\"obius coefficients and
two paired six-prime weights.  The separate-vector trilinear architecture of
Bettin--Chandee \cite{BettinChandee2018} and the bilinear non-abelian
architecture of Pascadi \cite{Pascadi2025} do not, without a further legal
compression, accept this complete physical tensor.

Opening one labelled prime from each target gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:prime-open}
 B_t=pm,\qquad B_{t+h}=p'm',
\end{equation}
and the exact identities
\begin{equation}\label{eq:prime-open-identities}
        p'm'-pm=kh,
 \qquad d_tp'm'-d_{t+h}pm=2h.
\end{equation}
The finite labelled-choice cost is $6^2=36$, but the remaining coefficient
system still has two M\"obius variables, two exposed primes, two five-prime
coefficients, and the $A,k,h$ determinant average.  No current cited theorem has
been verified to accept all of those data literally.
